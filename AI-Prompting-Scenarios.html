Scenario 1 of 3
The Lesson Plan

Ms. Osei teaches Year 8 Samoan, a community language class. Her students come from a mix of families who speak Samoan at home and families who are learning it for the first time, and she is planning a unit on oral storytelling traditions.

Before opening a chat, she stops to think it through. AI could help her structure the lessons, but the stories themselves and how they are told need to come from her own knowledge and her students' families, not from the AI.

Her first message: "I'm planning a five-lesson Year 8 Samoan language unit on oral storytelling traditions. My class is a mix of heritage speakers and beginners, and I want to draw on the specific stories and dialects represented in my classroom rather than generic examples. My teaching style is discussion-based, with small group work. My goal is a five-lesson outline I can adapt. To begin, please functionally decompose the task."
